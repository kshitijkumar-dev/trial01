⌨️ Operational Guide & Universal Accessibility

Universal Accessibility Features: This simulation meets WCAG AAA universal standards. It is 100% playable without sight (built-in speech synthesizer + ARIA live announcements), without hearing (every audio broadcast appears in the synchronized live captions), and without speaking (pure single-key keyboard or mouse interaction).

Keyboard Shortcuts:

  • 1 to 4 : Choose decisions & select suspects
  • Enter : Confirm selection & progress
  • N : Advance to next round when consequence is displayed
  • R : Re-read current scenario, telemetry, & options
  • E : Open / Close Forensic Evidence Locker
  • S : Mute / Unmute speech synthesis
  • Esc : Close any open window
