BANKSHIFT Accessibility & Keyboard Controls

Universal Design Philosophy: BANKSHIFT is 100% playable without sight (using speech narration and screen readers), without hearing (every event is visually subtitled and annotated), and without speech (no voice commands needed).

  • 1, 2, 3, 4 : Choose decisions
  • Enter or Space : Confirm selected decision
  • N : Proceed to next round
  • R : Repeat round description and choices aloud
  • S : Toggle Voice Speech Synthesis
  • Esc or H : Close this window

You can also use a standard mouse or touch screen at any time.
